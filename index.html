Community Integrations

Open-source projects that talk to APIMaster. The ones marked community are built and maintained by their own authors — they are not our products, and each card links to the author's repository.

Submit your integration

New projects are welcome; star count is not a criterion. We look for a public repo with a working README, a license, real calls to an OpenAI-compatible endpoint, and a commit in the last six months. Open an issue →

Our commitments

  • If you want your project's description changed, or the listing removed, we do it the same day — no questions.
  • If our endpoints or parameters change, we open the follow-up pull request in your repo ourselves.
  • Star counts are fetched on a schedule and shown with the date they were read. We do not inflate them.
